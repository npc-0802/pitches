What doing nothing costs

Every month the plan waits defers about $350K of marketplace revenue. Paid search is getting dearer: cost per visit rose 17% from June to August 2026 ($0.78 to $0.91), about $2.0M a month more for August's 15.1M paid visits. Referring domains fell 16% in a year (60,906 to 51,037), and Google AI Overviews sit on 78% of CarGurus' top-6 rankings, so holding a position earns fewer clicks each year. Pages take 3–6 months to rank: work started now lands before the February–April 2027 tax-refund season (in 2026, March visits jumped 17% on February).
