What Row House's search, AI answers and the Google Ads it already runs are worth in new online orders

Row House gets about 495 search visits a month, down 61% in a year while Purl Soho fell 31%, and it's in the top 100 for 1 of the 46 yarn and kit searches US shoppers make (~164,210 a month). Row House already runs Google Ads, but its collection pages are titled "Bulky Weight" or "All Kits", Google reads a $5 yarn as "$500", and AI answers name Row House in ~1% of shopping questions. What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about $0 of new online sales at these rates. Row House's search visits are down 61% in a year, against 31% for Purl Soho and 43% for Michigan Fine Yarns: if December follows October's trend, Row House enters gift season ~750 search visits a month behind last year. Meanwhile Google shows "Sale price $500 Regular price $600" for a $5 Erika Knight yarn, the 64 kits sit at /collections/beginner-kits under "All Kits", and AI answers send gift and kit shoppers to Etsy and We Are Knitters.

Demand and rankings: Ahrefs, US, Oct 22, 2026: 46 national yarn, kit, gift and learning searches, 164,210 a month (93,210 without the generic "yarn"); Row House is in the top 100 for 1 ("organic cotton yarn", #1). Visits: half of the ~375 shopping visits a month Ahrefs saw lost; half of 2,270 monthly striking-distance category searches into the top three; half of the 67,180 weight and fiber searches and all 11,180 kit, gift and learning searches reaching page one (~3.5% of clicks after an AI Overview haircut). Ceiling: Michigan Fine Yarns' traffic (12,695 visits a month at DR 29). Ads: Ahrefs guide CPCs; ads win ~15% of a market's clicks (ROAST assumption), so the shopping searches allow ~13,982 clicks a month (~$4,195 at $0.30). Existing ads: Google Ads Transparency Center, Oct 22, 2026. AI: Peec, Oct 22, 2026 (day one; 94 prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews); GWI Core, Q1 2025–Q2 2026 (US fine art & handicraft enthusiasts, a proxy).
