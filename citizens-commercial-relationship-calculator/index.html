What’s at stake

48% of US business decision-makers find new suppliers through search (index 146), and ~59% say Gen AI answers sway their supplier research (GWI, a proxy for commercial banking buyers). 45% of B2B buyers have used GenAI, mainly to research vendors (Gartner, May 20, 2026). Among US firms with $1–500M in revenue, 68% cite referrals and 44% use bank websites when they engage banking partners, and 46% are considering switching a bank in the next 12 months (Coalition Greenwich + LinkedIn, Q2 2025). Those buyers search ~67,800 times a month for the commercial products Citizens sells, and the clicks go to JPMorgan, Bank of America, PNC, KeyBank and local rivals: Citizens ranks in Google’s top three for none of those searches, and only ~820 of the ~5,500 Google clicks a month to its commercial pages aren’t clients logging in. At these rates the plan you pick is worth about $0 a month in new first-year relationship revenue once the work is live.

How the visits are modeled: each search’s volume × the share of clicks its Google position earns (28%, 16% and 11% for the top three, ~4.2% for positions 4–10), halved because every commercial results page we checked carried an AI Overview. Phase 1 takes half of the 13 searches where Citizens’ commercial pages already rank #8–#40 into the top three. Phase 2’s new pages reach page one for 30% of the rest and its market pages win 40% of city searches ("asset based lending banks boston"; placeholder at 12% of the national volume until Keyword Planner city volumes arrive). Phase 3 moves that 30% into the top three and adds ~100 visits a month from AI assistants. Sets: Phase 1 only ~330 visits a month, Phases 1 and 2 ~1,010, all three phases ~2,400.
Formula: new first-year relationship revenue a year = new buyer visits a month × 12 × visit to inquiry × commercial-size share × inquiry to relationship × first-year revenue per relationship (a year once the work is live, by month 12, not a ramped first year), + the search ads test: budget a month ÷ cost per click × months × visit to inquiry × the ads’ commercial-size share × inquiry to relationship × first-year revenue per relationship. Revenue is net interest income and fees in a relationship’s first year, before funding, credit and operating costs; what the same visits would cost in Google Ads isn’t added. Revenue per $1 of plan = all of it ÷ (the draft fee for the phases picked: Phase 1 only ~$50K, Phases 1 and 2 ~$144K, all three phases ~$266K; + the ad media + the test’s fees, ~$23K for set-up, three months of management and the week-12 read-out, then ~$4.3K a month of management after month 3). Move the visits slider yourself and the fee is the smallest set of phases that reaches those visits (all three above ~2,400).
