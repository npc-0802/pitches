What doing nothing costs

Every month the plan waits pushes back about $41K of new-project fees at run-rate. Meanwhile 6,780 category searches a month (about 81,000 a year) go to Little, MG2, Miller Zell, BHDP and the VMSD list. Organic traffic is down 74% since June 2025 and fell another 15% from August to September (Similarweb's September run-rate is 17% below August). On the 15-month trend, it reaches about 110 visits a month by September 2027. Until the homepage ranks again, a referred prospect who searches "bergmeyer" sees an obituary first. Similarweb puts about 730 name-search visits a month behind that result. AI assistants already send about 2% of visits, yet ChatGPT cites Bergmeyer zero times for category questions, so the AI shortlist is forming without it.
