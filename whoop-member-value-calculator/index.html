What's at stake

People choose a tracker in Google ~247K times a month and ask health questions WHOOP measures ~1.7M times (Similarweb, US). Google’s AI Overview for “best fitness tracker” names Google Fitbit Air the best screenless tracker, the category WHOOP started, and doesn’t name WHOOP; on page one for “screenless fitness tracker”, Reddit recommends WHOOP but whoop.com isn’t there. ChatGPT’s five best trackers leave WHOOP out, and its HRV answer puts Oura first. Oura is cited in ~10.5K AI answers to WHOOP’s ~7.1K (ChatGPT 535 vs 269) and buys ~458K paid search visits a month to WHOOP’s ~68K. WHOOP’s Google visits fell ~106K a month over six months (Ahrefs). At these rates the plan you pick is worth about $0 a month in first-year membership once the work is live.
