What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the plan forgoes about $63,000 of online revenue (525 orders). Meanwhile Ahrefs estimates Talbots' rankings earn 49% less traffic than a year ago (428K to 220K visits a month, Sep 2025 to Sep 2026), and sort, filter and paging URLs rank in place of category pages for ~260+ searches. With ~90% of search visits on its own name, any dip in brand demand reaches search with nothing underneath it. Separately, ~96,000 paid clicks a month go to the search "talbots", where Talbots already ranks #1 (~$56K a month at Similarweb's estimated CPC); a holdout test would show how much of that is defensive. No saving is counted here.

For Talbots to validate

  • Visit-to-order rate on non-branded search visits (placeholder 1.5%) and average order value (placeholder $120).
  • Non-branded organic visits today: our rank model says ~65,000 a month; Similarweb's brand split implies ~89,000. Search Console settles it.
  • Search ad spend: Similarweb estimates ~$356K a month ($450K in June, $335K in July, $283K in August 2026), with 84% of visible paid clicks on Talbots' own name. Ahrefs sees ~$129 a month: it only logs an ad if Google showed it when Ahrefs crawled that search, so its paid estimate swings and undercounts; Similarweb sees paid search every week from 1 Jun to 3 Oct. The account or the Ads Transparency Center settles it.
  • Non-branded Shopping ROAS: at a 1.82% visit-to-order rate, or CPCs of $0.90 or less, a non-branded click earns 2x its cost and category ads join the plan.
Where the tools disagree: Similarweb counts ~927K organic search visits a month (every engine, ~90% brand); Ahrefs models ~253K US from Google rankings (51% of it the word "talbots").
