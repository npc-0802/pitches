What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the plan forgoes about $72,000 of online revenue (600 orders). Meanwhile the pages that need to climb are losing support: Talbots' referring domains fell from 7,852 to 5,378 in twelve months (−32%), about 200 a month, and backlinks fell 65% (Similarweb, Oct 2025 to Oct 2026). With 85–92% of search visits on its own name, any dip in brand demand reaches search with nothing underneath it. Separately, ~96,000 paid clicks a month go to the search "talbots", where Talbots already ranks #1 (~$56K a month at Similarweb's estimated CPC); a holdout test would show how much of that is defensive. No saving is counted here.
