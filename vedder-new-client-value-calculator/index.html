What waiting costs

Old vedderprice.com addresses still answer with temporary (302) redirects, and 125 sites, including Wikipedia, Forbes and Cornell Law, link to Vedder pages that now return 404. Chambers' profile still reads "Vedder Price" and links to vedderprice.com, while Google's AI names ten equipment finance firms without Vedder. GC teams' use of gen AI doubled in a year, from 44% to 87% (FTI 2026). Each month without the plan defers about $0 of first-year fees at these rates.

Search demand: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, US, 42 head terms buyers of outside counsel use for Vedder's practices (11,820 searches a month; Ahrefs undercounts the B2B legal long tail, so this is a floor). Search today: ~80 category and topic clicks a month to vedder.com (Ahrefs, 15 Oct 2026). Before the move vedderprice.com ranked for 895 terms; vedder.com ranks for 200. AI: Peec, ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, 110 prompts, 888 answers, 15 Oct 2026 (day one): Vedder named in 5.7% of answers to the 92 prompts that don't name it. AI buyer questions are a proxy: one per search × 70% of GC teams using gen AI for general queries (FTI/Relativity General Counsel Report 2026, n=224, via Global Legal Post). Ads: 27 terms, 8,640 searches a month, volume-weighted CPC $7.99 (Ahrefs), capped at 15% of those searches. Directories: Chambers USA 2026, Chambers Global 2026, Legal 500 US 2026.
Formula: buyer visits = searches × reach + AI questions × share naming Vedder × 10%, counted only above today's ~127 a month. Ad clicks = ads ÷ $7.99.
