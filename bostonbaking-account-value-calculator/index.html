What doing nothing costs

Every month the plan waits pushes back about $27K of new account revenue at run-rate. PLMA runs 15–17 November in Chicago: buyers who meet Boston Baking there will search for it afterwards and find a site that never says "wholesale" and mentions "private label" on one page the homepage doesn't link to. Genuine backlinks fell from 277 to 146 between October 2025 and September 2026 (Similarweb), and non-brand search has sat at 6–17 visits a month since October 2025, its lowest on record (Ahrefs; peak 123 in January 2022). Bake'n Joy, Bellows House and Shirley's lead with the words buyers type. AI assistants already send about 27 visits a month, yet Ahrefs finds Boston Baking cited only 5 times across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot, so the AI shortlist is forming without it. With capex doubled and 10,000 sq ft being added, new capacity needs new accounts.
