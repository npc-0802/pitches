What’s at stake

About 9.7M Americans own a vacation home (GWI), and they hear about new services through AI tools at 1.85x the average rate. On day one of tracking, AI named Evolve in 50% of unbranded owner answers, and in 0 of 14 “manager or DIY?” answers, the self-managers Evolve most wants to convert. What AI says about Evolve comes largely from Tidy (which pitches 3.9%), RedAwning, Awning and Reddit, and Google’s AI tells owners Evolve adds “an $80 monthly fee” its fee FAQ doesn’t list. At these rates the plan is worth about $0 a month in new first-year fees at run-rate, and blended cost per qualified lead falls to $0 on the same media. Peec reads the same 72 prompts every day, so the trend is measurable from the first week.

Audience: GWI USA, Q3 2025–Q2 2026, US internet users who say they own a vacation home (n=3,037; universe ~9.7M): used ChatGPT last month 37.5% (index 121), Gemini 20.7% (121), review sites 15.1% (139), search engines 61.7% (77); typically hear about new products through AI search tools 11.0% (185, n=168). AI visibility: Peec, Evolve project, day one (Nov 2, 2026), 72 prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews; Evolve named in 50.4% of 238 answers on 40 unbranded owner prompts; a single day, so read prompt-level numbers as directional. Media: Evolve’s Senior Manager, Performance Marketing posting (Greenhouse, read Nov 2, 2026): an eight-figure annual media budget for owner acquisition, run in-house. Site: Similarweb, US, Jul–Sep 2026: ~540K visits a month (guests and owners), ~14K to owner pages; est. Google search spend ~$151K a month. Fees: evolve.com (Core 10%, Plus 15%, Pro custom, $250 onboarding).
Formula: owner media ÷ cost per qualified lead = qualified leads a month. AI answers: leads × share of owner demand that starts in AI × (share of AI owner answers naming Evolve with the plan − 50% today) = extra leads. Owner pages: leads × lift = extra leads. Extra leads × lead-to-signed-home = new homes; × 12 × first-year fees per home = new fees a year; × years a home stays = fees over the homes’ stay. Budget re-aimed = media × 12 × share in new channels × share re-aimed; its homes = re-aimed ÷ cost per lead × lead-to-signed; never added to the fees.
