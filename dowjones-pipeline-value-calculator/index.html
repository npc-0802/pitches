What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about $0 of new first-year contract value at these rates. Meanwhile 345 websites still link to 64 old Dow Jones risk & compliance pages that now show as broken, 149 of them to the old KYC guide, and their authority goes nowhere. In AI answers, LSEG's pages are cited more than twice as often as Dow Jones' (288 vs 120 citations in a day), and Google's AI answer for "best sanctions screening data providers for banks" cites LSEG's page to describe Dow Jones. Buyers are shopping: Risk & Compliance revenue grew 16% last year.

Demand and rankings: Ahrefs, US, 15 Oct 2026: 33 risk & compliance and news-data head terms, 61,560 searches a month (12-month average), 18,860 on 21 solution searches and 42,700 on 12 definition and list searches; Dow Jones top 10 for 3 (live on 15 Oct, Dow Jones blog posts also ranked #8 for "pep screening" and #9 for "adverse media screening"; LexisNexis 16, Moody's 8, LSEG 7). Head terms only, US only: the long tail and other markets are extra. CTR curve (ROAST working curve): #1 28%, #2 16%, #3 11%, top-three average with a 50% AI Overview haircut ~9.2%. Ads: Ahrefs CPC guide (volume-weighted $6.24); ads rarely win more than ~15% of a market's clicks (~$17,500 a month on these searches) until Keyword Planner volumes arrive. Broken links: Ahrefs, 414 linking sites to 112 dead /professional/ URLs. AI: Peec.ai, 15 Oct 2026 (one day, 76 prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews): non-branded visibility Dow Jones 47.3% vs LSEG 54.7%, named first 4.6% vs 18.4%. GWI Core + Work (Q1 2025–Q3 2026, US banking/financial-services decision-makers, a proxy for compliance buyers): 80% find search results influential in vendor research, 67% Gen AI answers, 30% "very." Revenue: News Corp FY2026 results (Aug 2026).
Formula: (solution visits + AI referrals) × solution demo rate + glossary visits × glossary demo rate = organic demo requests a month; budget ÷ CPC × click-to-demo = ad demo requests; demo requests × 12 × demo-to-customer = new customers a year; × first-year contract value = revenue.
