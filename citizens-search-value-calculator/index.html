What waiting costs

Every month the plan waits defers about $103,950 of first-year account value to banks and publishers that rank above Citizens. Paid search is getting dearer: Similarweb estimates Citizens' cost per paid visit rose from $2.21 in June to $3.88 in August 2026, and monthly spend from $0.48M to $0.90M. AI Overviews already sit on 98% of the product searches in this model, and the top-result click penalty Ahrefs measured grew from 34.5% (April 2025) to 58% (February 2026); pages that aren't the cited source lose the most.

Traffic: Similarweb, US, Jun–Aug 2026 and Sep 2025 – Aug 2026 (citizensbank.com ~5.3M visits and ~1.7M organic visits a month; 88–91% of search visits branded; Rank Distribution of 26,391 keywords, Aug 2026; competitor compares pulled 2 Oct 2026). Rankings, backlinks and AI citations: Ahrefs, 30 Sep 2026 (Ahrefs and Similarweb aren't blended). Economics: Citizens Financial Group Q2 2026 results (net interest margin 3.17%, average deposits $183.6B, consumer deposits 64% of total). AI Overview click penalty: Ahrefs studies, April 2025 and February 2026.
Formula: (product-page visits a month × application rate + learning visits a month × learning application rate) × funded rate × 12 × first-year value per account. Visits: 3,092 non-branded product searches Citizens ranks for, 30% of the volume at #4–30 moved to #3, #6 or #10; click-through 28% at #1, 10% at #3, 4% at #6, 1.7% at #10, cut 58% where an AI Overview appears. Year one assumes a linear six-month ramp. Paid equivalent is what the visits would cost in Google Ads at $13.13 a click (Similarweb click costs for these terms, weighted by the gain), not what they earn, and the two are never added. Every value uses placeholder rates until Citizens shares its own; this is an illustrative model, not a forecast or a promise about rates, approvals or rankings.
