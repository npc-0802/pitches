What's at stake

60% of US dance and exercise class-goers research on search engines, half read consumer reviews and 1 in 5 already use AI tools like ChatGPT (GWI, a proxy for SoulCycle’s riders). They search for a spin or cycling class ~42,000 times a month. Where they choose a studio, Google’s map pack shows studios rated 4.8–5.0, several with 2–15x the reviews; SoulCycle’s have ~70, rated 4.1–4.8, and for “best spin class” in Boston and LA none of its 7 studios makes the pack. Google’s AI and ChatGPT cite Barry’s and [solidcore]’s sites ~1,100 times each, and SoulCycle’s 549 (Ahrefs). At these rates the plan you pick is worth about $0 a month in new rider revenue once the work is live.
