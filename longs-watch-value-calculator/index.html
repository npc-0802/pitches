What waiting costs

Every month without the plan defers about $55,319 of watch sales and service at these rates. Meanwhile Long's search visits are down 36% on last year, in line with Shreve, Crump & Low (−44%), Mayors (−43%) and Lux Bond & Green (−34%), so Long's heads into December, its busiest search month, ~14,900 visits behind if the trend holds (Ahrefs, Oct 21, 2026). The market moved for everyone, so the way back is a bigger share of watch searches, which today bring under 7% of Long's search clicks.

Demand: Ahrefs, US, Oct 21, 2026. 178,670 monthly watch searches mapped (dealer, brand + Boston, pre-owned and service terms); Long's is in the top 10 for 6,230 of them (3.5%). Watch pages bring ~1,346 search visits a month today. Store-area share: the Boston and Manchester-Nashua metros hold ~5.3M of ~335M people in the US (~1.6%), our estimate for Long's six stores. Click-through: an Advanced Web Ranking-style curve (top three average 17.5%, 11.5% where an AI Overview shows; positions 4–10 average ~3.5% after the AI Overview haircut). Live checks from Lexington, MA on Oct 21, 2026: Google Maps for "watch repair near me" showed Jewelry Rapid Repair, Angela's Jewelry & Watch Repair and Watertown Watch & Clock Repair; "certified pre owned rolex" showed rolex.com, Mayors, Heller, Watches of Switzerland, Betteridge, The 1916 Company and Bucherer. Google's Ads Transparency Center lists ~600 ads to longsjewelers.com from two advertisers, Long's Jewelers and Rolex Industries, Inc.
