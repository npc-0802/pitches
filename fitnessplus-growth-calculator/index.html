What the plan is worth, and what it needs from the credit line

About 4,500 people in the US visit fitnessplus.com each month. Its category pages don't rank, a handful of product pages carry organic search, and even the smallest competitor gets five times its organic visits. The plan rebuilds the site with a fix-in-place bridge, grows organic toward real competitors, and tests paid media for 90 days before scaling it. Every FitnessPlus rate below is a placeholder until the owner confirms it.

The gate: at the end of the 90-day test, paid steps up if its return covers media, the management fee and the revenue share at your margin (the scale bar), stops if it can't cover media and the share (the stop bar), and holds at the test budget in between.
Timing: rebuild in parallel puts tracking and product-page fixes live in months 1–3, paid from month 2 and the rebuilt site in month 5; rebuild first holds everything new until month 5; fix in place drops the rebuild and its extra 15 points of conversion.
