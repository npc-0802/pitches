What fixing the site and funding the plan is worth

About 4,500 people in the US visit fitnessplus.com each month. Its category pages don't rank, so a handful of product pages carry organic search, and dealers with the same authority get 2.4 to 3.7 times the organic traffic. There is no structured paid search program. The plan closes that gap. Every rate below starts as a placeholder until FitnessPlus confirms its own number.

added revenue/month = US visits × r × lift + added organic × r × (1 + lift) + (media ÷ CPC) × r × 0.75, where r = inquiry rate × close rate × order value
Traffic: Similarweb, US, June–August 2026 (4,502 visits a month; 1,445 organic in August) and Ahrefs, 30 September 2026 (901 US organic visits a month; peers from its organic competitor report). International visits (75% of the total, mostly under 25 seconds) are left out.
Timing: Tier 1's 10-point conversion lift lands over months 1–3 and the rest over months 4–12 (at launch, then months 6–14, when rebuilding). Organic gains ramp from month 3 to full in month 12 (month 6 to 15 when rebuilding). Paid media starts at half budget in month 2 (month 5). Orders book one month after the inquiry. A paid click converts at 75% of the site average. Levers are not double-counted across scenarios.
"Gross profit minus cost" is added gross profit less the program's fee, media, build and revenue share. It is before freight, sales time on the extra inquiries and the cost of credit.
