What doing nothing costs

Every month the plan waits pushes back about $99K of new-engagement fees at run-rate. Meanwhile 6,170 buyer searches a month (about 74,000 a year) go to KEG, Carnegie, Boston agencies and listicles. Organic search traffic is down 79% in a year (Ahrefs: 971 visits in September 2025, 203 in September 2026), backlinks fell 28% in October 2025, and 81 domains rated DR 30+ still link to pages that return 404. AI assistants already send more visits than every referring website combined, yet Upstatement appears in 4% of higher-ed answers against Carnegie's 36%, while public universities scope accessibility-driven redesigns ahead of the 26 April 2027 ADA Title II deadline. A flagship site that goes to a competitor isn't re-tendered for years.
