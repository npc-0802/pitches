What Google Ads and form pages are worth in disclosure sales for Onward

Every California divorce needs a financial disclosure from each spouse within 60 days of filing: ~214,292 a year. When they search for the forms (FL-150, FL-142, "how to file for divorce in California"), Google shows the courts' free PDFs and an AI answer that cites Hello Divorce's videos. Onward gets no visits from the ~12,802 searches a month, and no ads ran on the two we checked live. This models Google Ads on the searches where a click earns its cost, the form pages the ads land on, and those pages ranking. Pick a tier, then replace our placeholder rates with Onward's own.

What waiting costs

Each month without ads or form pages forgoes about $0 of disclosure sales at these rates, about 0 buyers. They don't come back later: their disclosure is due 60 days after filing, so they finish it with the courts' free PDF, a paralegal or a lawyer at ~$415 an hour. Meanwhile Hello Divorce buys ~32,081 search clicks a month and its videos are what Google's AI answer cites for the FL-140 and FL-150, while Onward got ~99 paid search visits in September.

Searches: Similarweb keyword generator, US, 12-month average to Sep 2026, pulled 29 Oct 2026: 15 disclosure-form searches (FL-150, FL-142, FL-140 and "declaration of disclosure" variants), 6,134 a month; 22 California DIY-divorce searches, 6,668 a month. Ads buy the 10,316 where a click earns at least twice its cost at the default rates (CPC up to $3.73); ads rarely win more than ~15% of a market's clicks (~1,547 a month here). CTR curve (ROAST working curve): #1 28%, #2 16%, #3 11%, top-three average with a 50% AI Overview haircut ~9.2%. Market: 107,146 California marital filings in FY 2024–25 (Judicial Council of California, Court Statistics Report 2026); each spouse serves a Preliminary Declaration of Disclosure within 60 days of filing (Family Code §2104). Site traffic: Similarweb, US, Jul–Sep 2026: ~1,880 visits a month, ~473 from organic search, 99 from paid search in September; Hello Divorce ~32,081 paid search visits a month. Audience: GWI Core, USA, Q1 2025–Q2 2026 (proxy: divorced or widowed Americans): 64% mainly research brands through search engines (index 111), 11% through AI tools (index 68).
Formula: Google Ads budget ÷ CPC (capped at ~15% of the searches) × ad click to purchase = buyers from ads; Google visits × page visit to purchase = buyers from the pages; × 12 = a year; × price = disclosure sales. Sales per $1 of ads = ad buyers × price ÷ ad budget. What the page clicks would cost in Google Ads is never added to the sales.
