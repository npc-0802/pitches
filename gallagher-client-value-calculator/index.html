What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the work defers about $84,000 of first-year commission & fees. Ahrefs models Gallagher's organic visits down ~40% in a year (133.5K to 80.8K a month, all countries), or ~4,400 visits a month on average. Six buyer pages slipped in August 2026 alone (Similarweb): "hr consulting" from #8 to #34 and "commercial insurance broker" from #10 to #20. Meanwhile an estimated 73% of Gallagher's paid search clicks buy its own name, which it already ranks #1 for.

Traffic: Similarweb, US (rank distribution and website keywords, Jun–Aug 2026; marketing channels and search performance; pulled 5 Oct 2026); Ahrefs, 30 Sep 2026 (all countries). Visits are modelled from Similarweb's rank data: search volume × click-through at each position (#1 28%, #3 11%, #10 2%, page two 1%, pages three and four 0.3%), with a 12% top-3 click share where an AI Overview shows (28 of 32 results). Today's ~700 visits a month on these searches are worth ~$252K a year at the default rates. Buyer context: GWI Core (Q1 2025–Q2 2026) and GWI Work (Q3 2026), US business decision-makers, a proxy audience: 48% find new suppliers through search; 59% say AI answers influence supplier research.
Formula: (new buyer-search visits + new-page and AI visits) × enquiry rate × enquiry-to-client = new clients a month; × 12 × first-year commission & fees per client = first-year revenue a year. Year one: pages ramp over six months. The paid-equivalent (visits × cost per click) is what buying the same clicks would cost; it isn't added to revenue. Every revenue figure uses placeholder rates until Gallagher shares its own; brand, careers, client-login and student-plan traffic are excluded.
