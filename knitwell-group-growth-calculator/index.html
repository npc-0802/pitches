What's at stake

Search engines are how ~3 in 10 US women 45–64 discover brands (31%, GWI USA, Q3 2025–Q2 2026). Across the group they make ~35.9M non-branded searches a month that a KnitWell site ranks for, and KnitWell is in the top three for ~7%; today those searches bring the seven sites ~947K Google visits a month. ~78% of the group’s search visits are people who already typed a KnitWell name. 2,874 searches (8.6M a month) have two or more sister brands sitting at #4–20 together, splitting the signal so neither breaks into the top three. Similarweb estimates the group spends ~$3.4M a month on search ads, and ~81% of the paid clicks it can see are on each brand’s own name, where it already ranks #1. Once it is live, the plan is worth ~$0 a month at these rates.

Formula: new Google visits = each phase’s share of the lift on the searches each brand owns (owned searches at #4–10 into the top three, page-two searches to #5) × the lift slider; orders = Google visits × visit-to-order + AI visits × visit-to-order × 1.3; sales = orders × each brand’s order value × 12. AI visits = today’s × the AI slider. Year one counts the work as it lands (Phase 1 from month 3, Phase 2 from month 5, Phase 3 from month 6). Placeholder rates; before cost of goods and returns.
