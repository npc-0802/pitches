What winning back lost searches is worth in new Industrious memberships

Since November 2025, Industrious has dropped out of the top 10 for 193 generic searches like "office space near me", and its homepage went from 486 ranking keywords to 44 (Similarweb). Brand searches held up; the loss is the ~17,000 non-branded visits a month (up to ~27,000 by Ahrefs) that bring in people who don't know Industrious yet. Meanwhile Google Ads spend rose an estimated 75% in a year. Pick a tier, then replace our placeholder rates with Industrious' own.

What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the fix defers about $514,853 of first-year membership revenue. In the meantime the bill for buying the demand back keeps rising: Similarweb estimates Industrious' Google Ads spend at ~$658K in August 2026, ~$282K a month more than in September 2025 (+75%), with cost per paid visit up 17%. Ahrefs models organic falling by ~2,600 visits a month on average across the same year, in steps (November 2025, spring 2026, September 2026). And each of the ~50 new locations Industrious planned for 2026 opens without the non-branded visibility the old site had.
