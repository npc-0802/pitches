What winning the shoppers still choosing is worth to Purple in new online orders

About four in five of Purple's search visits already know its name, and an estimated 90% of its search-ad spend buys that name (Similarweb). The shoppers still choosing ask "best mattress for side sleepers", "best cooling mattress" or "queen bed dimensions": across 48 of the biggest mattress, pillow and bedding searches (1.84M a month), Purple is in Google's top 100 for 3, and the AI answers name Helix, Brooklyn Bedding, Nectar and Leesa. This calculator moves part of the brand budget onto those searches, adds the pages and AI answers ads can't buy, and counts only new orders. What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about $0 of new online sales at these rates. Meanwhile an estimated 90% of Purple's search-ad spend keeps buying its own name into Black Friday and Presidents' Day, the AI Overviews for side sleepers, cooling and back pain name Helix, Brooklyn Bedding, Nectar and Saatva, and rivals' ads read "Compare … to Purple". Google shows an AI Overview on 49 of 52 mattress head-term searches, so the clicks that are left go to the brands those answers name.

Demand and rankings: Ahrefs, US, Oct 23, 2026: 48 non-branded mattress, pillow, bedding and base searches, 1,835,390 a month; Purple is in the top 100 for 3. Visits: half of the 330,600 monthly "best mattress for…" and type searches reaching page one at ~3.5% of clicks after an AI Overview haircut; half the striking-distance upside (Ahrefs' curve to position 3, untested); a third of lost page traffic won back (the whole sector lost clicks to AI answers); queen, twin and full size pages for 599,900 monthly searches. Ceiling: page one for every shopping search. Search spend: Similarweb, US, Jul–Sep 2026 (~$1.51M a month modeled; ~90% of paid cost on brand terms in its keyword sample).
