What category search ads, rebuilt collections and AI answers are worth to Fairfax & Favor in new online orders

Brand searches still find Fairfax & Favor, but the boot, bag and outerwear searches a new customer makes have stopped landing on its site: on the same keywords, category search visits fell from ~21,149 last October to ~1,674 this October (−92%), and "knee high boots" (52,000 searches a month) slid from #7 off page one. Fairfax & Favor's search ads (about £14,000 in September, Similarweb's estimate) are aimed almost entirely at its own name, not these searches, and Google's AI answers for them name ASOS, Dune and Dubarry. What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about £0 of new online sales at these rates. On the same keywords, Fairfax & Favor goes into Black Friday and Christmas with ~19,500 fewer category search visits a month than last October, and its search ads are aimed at its own name, not the gap. Meanwhile Holland Cooper's brand searches grew 11% in a year while Fairfax & Favor's fell 13%, and Holland Cooper is cited in AI answers about twice as often (933 vs 425 UK citations).

Demand and rankings: Ahrefs, UK, 23 Oct 2026: 46 head category searches, 262,500 a month on a 12-month average (204,500 without "womens trainers"), plus 80 gap searches (200,950 a month) where two or more rivals rank and Fairfax & Favor doesn't. Visits: a quarter (Tier 1) and half (Tier 2) of the ~19,475 category visits lost between Oct 2025 and Oct 2026 on the same keywords, divided by the category searches' own October peak (×1.98) to an average month; half the striking-distance gain to the top three, leaving out keywords already counted as lost (4,460); half the non-knee-high gap searches reaching page one (~3.5% of clicks after an AI Overview haircut); half of four US page-one searches into the top three. The Oct 2025 level was itself a spike (+65% on Oct 2024), so the plan never counts all of it back below the ceiling. Ads: Similarweb UK CPCs for the category searches (Jul–Sep 2026); ads win ~15% of a market's clicks (ROAST assumption), so these searches allow ~58,208 clicks a month (~£26,194 at £0.45). AI: Ahrefs AI citation counts (Brand Radar not licensed) and four live AI Overviews, 23 Oct 2026; GWI Core, Q1 2025–Q2 2026 (UK women 35–64 who always buy premium, a proxy). Today's paid search: Similarweb, UK, Jul–Sep 2026 (modelled; 66 of 70 text ads' top keyword is the brand name, the other 4 Holland Cooper or Penelope Chilvers) and ~200 ads in Google's Ads Transparency Center, 23 Oct 2026. Money: Ahrefs and Similarweb report in US dollars, converted at £1 = $1.34 (23 Oct 2026).
