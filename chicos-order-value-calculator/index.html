What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the plan forgoes about $45,000 of online revenue (375 orders). Meanwhile the pages that need to climb are losing support: Chico's referring domains fell from 7,656 to 5,039 in twelve months (−34%), about 215 a month, and backlinks fell 36% (Similarweb, Oct 2025 to Oct 2026), while Ahrefs estimates its rankings earn 55% less traffic than a year ago. With 86% of search visits on its own name, any dip in brand demand reaches search with nothing underneath it. Separately, ~77,000 paid clicks a month go to the search "chicos", where Chico's already ranks #1 (~$41.5K a month at Similarweb's estimated CPC); a holdout test would show how much of that is defensive. No saving is counted here.
