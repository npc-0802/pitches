What more sign-ups per ad dollar and the search and AI traffic MarketBeat can still win are worth in new subscriber revenue

What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about $0 of new subscriber revenue at these rates. Meanwhile non-Google search fell a third last quarter (~598K visits in July, ~342K in September); even at a 20% yearly decline it loses ~98K visits a month within a year, about 980 sign-ups a month. Links from Forbes, Fortune and TheStreet still point at deleted pages that return 404, and referring domains fell 16% from November to their September low. On “tsla price target” and “earnings calendar,” Google’s AI Overviews quote TipRanks, Zacks, Yahoo and Nasdaq, not MarketBeat.
