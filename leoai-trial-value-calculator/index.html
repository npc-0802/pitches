Demand and rankings: Ahrefs, US, 20 Oct 2026: 27 clip, repurposing, short-form and scheduling terms, 6,430 searches a month (Leo in the top 100 for none); competitor names 83,800 a month (“opus clip” 68,000). Traffic potential (what the #1 page gets) for Leo’s 10 clip and repurposing parent topics: ~11,300 US visits a month; tiers take 2%, 8%, 20% and 60% of it, and 60% lands on the peer median (6,969: Opus Clip 60,646, Vizard 10,579, Submagic 9,655, Repurpose.io 4,283, Klap 1,867, 2short.ai 316). Ads: Ahrefs CPC guide (volume-weighted $0.97); we assume ads can win ~15% of the category’s searches and ~3% of rival-name searches (ROAST assumptions), so ~3,478 clicks a month (~$3,500 at $1.00). AI: Peec.ai, 20 Oct 2026 (day one; 91 prompts, 804 answers across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews).
