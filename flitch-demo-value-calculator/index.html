What's at stake

Lumberyards barely search Google for the category (~20 searches a month for “lumber yard software”); they search the systems they run, ~7.1K times a month, and ~1,390 of those are for BisTrack, whose final on-premises Desktop release is due in December 2026, with active support ending December 31, 2028 (Epicor). Rundoo has published 33 articles, including “The best POS systems for lumberyards in 2026”, and gets ~4x Flitch’s AI visits; Flitch has 4 articles and 0 G2 reviews.
