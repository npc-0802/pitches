What's at stake

1 in 5 UK women who buy premium already research brands with AI tools like ChatGPT (19%, index 144), and 1 in 4 in the US (26%, index 161; GWI). When they ask for Fairfax & Favor by name, ChatGPT names it ~95% of the time and often picks it (over Holland Cooper, and first for race-day boots). When they ask for knee-high boots, wide calves or boots that last, it names Dune, DuoBoots, Tricker's and others, and the sources it reads (Horse & Hound, Vogue, The Independent, Who What Wear) name Fairfax & Favor's rivals. Every one of the 7 ChatGPT answers we checked on 5 Nov carried a sponsored boot; none was Fairfax & Favor's. AI assistants send Holland Cooper ~6x Fairfax & Favor's visits today. At these rates the plan you pick is worth about £0 a month in new online sales once the work is live.
