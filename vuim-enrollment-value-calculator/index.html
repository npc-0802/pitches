What's at stake

Students research acupuncture school and the career about 7,300 times a month on Google (Ahrefs, US), and AI assistants now answer “which school should I consider” market by market. In New Jersey, every page-one result for “acupuncture school new jersey” is about ESATM, which calls itself “New Jersey’s 1st & Only Acupuncture School”; VUIM’s Ridgefield campus appears only in the map, with its Virginia number. Near Atlanta, Google’s AI calls Hanbit University “the primary option”. A year ago vuim.edu ranked for “how to become an acupuncturist” (#22) and “acupuncture school near me” (#16); today it ranks for neither, and its Google visits fell ~44% from August to October 2026 while ESATM’s site grew ~13% (Similarweb). Maryland University of Integrative Health has stopped enrolling acupuncture students, so DC-area students who look it up (~250 searches a month) need a new home.
