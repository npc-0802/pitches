What’s at stake

Americans search for stair lifts about 219K times a month (Ahrefs), and 69% of US adults 65+ start brand research on a search engine (GWI). On the 40 core searches, AmeriGlide and Lifeway take 35% of the clicks and Stannah 6%; Stannah’s 159 town and state pages draw ~480 Google visits a month between them; and on day one of tracking AI named Stannah in 0 of 27 answers to caregivers’ questions, though it names Stannah in 65% of US stairlift answers overall (Peec). Meanwhile 56% of Stannah’s US visits come from paid channels; Bruno earns 59% of its own from Google search (Similarweb). At these rates the plan is worth about $0 a month in installed sales at run-rate, and media cost per search lead falls from $0 to $0 on the same ad budget.

Search demand and rankings: Ahrefs, US database (pulled Nov 3, 2026): “stair lift” terms ~133K and “stairlift” terms ~86K searches a month (brand terms included); stannah.com ~11,470 US organic visits a month (~7,500 non-brand), Bruno ~14,371, AmeriGlide ~24,680, Lifeway ~48,002; traffic share on 40 core keywords (67K searches a month): AmeriGlide 19.5%, Lifeway 15.9%, Stannah 6.4%. Site traffic and channels: Similarweb, US, Jul–Sep 2026: ~15,547 visits a month; paid search ~3,079, display ~5,125, Google organic ~4,281; est. Google search spend ~$22.3K in July. Audience: GWI Core, Q1 2025–Q2 2026, US internet users 65+ (n=21,323): 68.5% mainly use search engines to research brands (index 118), 49.1% consumer reviews (122); GWI USA, Q3 2025–Q2 2026, US caregivers: 10.0% hear about new products through AI search tools (index 187, n=157). AI visibility: Peec, Stannah project, day one (Nov 2, 2026), 639 US chats on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews; a single day, so read prompt-level numbers as directional. Prices: stannah.com (Nov 3, 2026).
Formula: paid search visits × share that ask = leads from ads; × the plan’s ad-page lift = extra ad leads. New non-brand Google visits × share that ask = extra organic leads. Leads × assessments that install = installs; × average installed sale = sales. Media cost per lead = paid visits × cost per visit ÷ all search leads (ROAST fees not included). Visits added by tier come from keyword-level click curves calibrated on Stannah’s own Ahrefs traffic (e.g. “stair lift”, 14K a month, from #8 to #4).
