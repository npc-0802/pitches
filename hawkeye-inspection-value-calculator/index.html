What waiting costs

Every month the plan waits defers about $10,075 of inspections to peers with town pages. Pages need three to six months to rank, so pages live by January reach the March–June buying season; pages started in spring arrive for 2028. Meanwhile Hawkeye's genuine referring domains fell 24% between February and August 2026 (Similarweb), four other Hawkeye and Hawk inspection sites compete for the name, and Hawkeye's 226 Google reviews sit on a profile at its old address.
