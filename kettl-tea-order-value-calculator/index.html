What's at stake

57% of US monthly tea drinkers say search engines are one of the main ways they research products, and ~1 in 10 already discover brands through AI tools like ChatGPT (9.7%, index 114; GWI). They search for matcha, hojicha and teaware ~1.4M times a month; Kettl's pages win ~2,400 of those visits. When they ask ChatGPT for the best ceremonial matcha, it names Kettl's Hakusan, quoting Bon Appétit and a ~$45 tin that is sold out on kettl.co (Nov 10). Kettl gets 22% of the visits across five Japanese-tea stores (Kettl, Ippodo, Tezumi, Senbird, Sazen) but 9% of the visits AI assistants send them.
