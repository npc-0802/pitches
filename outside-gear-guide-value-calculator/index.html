What winning the top three for gear searches is worth to Outside

Outside ranks in Google for 1,549 gear-buying searches (1.10M a month), but reaches the top three for just 72 of them, and AI Overviews now show on 98%. Twenty-one Outside pages compete for running-shoe searches alone. This models what moving the page-one and page-two searches into the top three, plus visits from AI answers, is worth in affiliate, Outside+ and ad revenue. Buying the same 23,400 visits in Google Ads would cost ~$26,442 a month ($317,304 a year). A gear visit is worth ~$0.28 at these rates against a $1.13 click, so ads don't pay here: ranking does.

A click costs more than it earns at these rates; if Outside's real revenue per visit is higher, the case gets stronger: change the sliders.

What waiting costs

Each month without the plan leaves ~23,400 gear-search visits with the rivals that outrank Outside (~$6.6K of revenue at these rates; ~$26K at Google Ads prices). Black Friday (Nov 27) is the biggest gear-buying week of the year. Outside's AI referrals fell 16% from July to September while GearJunkie's rose 25% (Similarweb).

Assumptions & sources. Demand: the 1,549 gear-buying searches ("best", "review", "vs"; brand, credit-card, travel, food and health searches excluded) that outsideonline.com, including run. and velo., ranks for in Google's top 50, ~1.10M searches a month: 72 in the top three, 304 at positions 4–10, 438 at 11–20 and 735 at 21–50 (Similarweb Rank Distribution, US, Sep 2026). Visits are modeled from that rank data: search volume × click share at each position (#1 28%, #2 16%, #3 11%, #10 2%, page two 0.8%, positions 21–50 0.2%), halved where an AI Overview shows (98% of these searches), so the top three average ~9% (CTR curve with a 50% AI Overview haircut; Ahrefs, Feb 2026: −58% at position 1; Seer, Nov 2025: −61%). On that curve Outside gets ~8,200 gear-search visits a month today, worth ~$28K a year at the default rates. Similarweb Website Keywords, Marketing Channels and Engagement (US, Jul–Sep 2026; outsideonline.com incl. run. and velo.) give the AI referrals (~12K visits a month, 0.39% of visits; OutdoorGearLab 0.94%), the ~1.8 pages a visit and the CPC: the visit-weighted Google Ads CPC of the 4–20 gear searches that have one. Rates. Every rate is a placeholder until Outside shares its own: retailer click-out, earnings per click (affiliate commission terms: REI 5%, Backcountry 8%), Outside+ join rate (Piano subscription benchmark, Digiday, 2022), member value (Outside+ pricing: $62.99 intro first year, $89.99 renewal; outsideonline.com/membership, Oct 12, 2026) and ad RPM.
Tiers. 1 = half of the 304 searches at 4–10 reach the top three; 2 (headline) = half of the 742 at 4–20; 3 = 75% of 4–20 and a quarter of 21–50, plus 5,000 AI visits (placeholder); 4 (ceiling) = all of 4–20 and half of 21–50, plus 10,000 AI visits. Not counted: Backpacker, Trail Runner, Ski, Climbing and Yoga Journal; news and feature traffic; ROAST fees.
Formula. Visits = new gear-search visits + AI visits. Revenue a year = visits × 12 × (click-out × earnings per click + join rate × member value + ad RPM ÷ 1,000). Retailer clicks a month = visits × click-out; Outside+ joins a year = visits × 12 × join rate. Ad-equivalent a month = new gear-search visits × CPC; it is what buying the same clicks would cost and is never added to revenue. Each month of waiting = revenue a year ÷ 12. The tiers change only the gear-search and AI visits; the rates stay as set.
