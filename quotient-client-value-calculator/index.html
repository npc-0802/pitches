What doing nothing costs

August organic ran about 1,400 visits a month below July's peak (3,116 → 1,734, Similarweb). At the rates above that is 4.9 fewer consultations a month, 12 fewer clients a year and $700K in lifetime fees per year the run-rate holds. Google AI Overviews now sit on every keyword Quotient ranks for and cite it zero times, and Dallas AI answers are being written from D Magazine, Wealthtender, the CFP Board directory and Google reviews — lists Quotient is not on yet. Firms that get onto those lists first tend to stay in the answer.

Traffic: Similarweb, US, Jun–Aug 2026 (organic 1,887 / 3,116 / 1,734; ~2,000–3,500 prospect visits a month once client logins are excluded — an inference, to confirm in GA4).
