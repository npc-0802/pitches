What waiting costs

The Oct 11 game against Cleveland drew 67,418 to an 82,500-seat stadium: about 15,082 empty seats, or ~$0.94M a game even at today's $62 median cheapest seat. The Giants drew 79,900 to 83,300 in the same building. 6 home Sundays are left this season, and each month without the plan defers about $0 of new revenue at these rates. Meanwhile, Google's AI tells visitors the Jets "average $370+ per ticket" and puts MetLife suites at $13,000 to $15,000 and up.

Demand: Similarweb keyword list (US, average monthly searches Oct 2025–Sep 2026, read 15 Oct 2026): ~133,246 "things to do in NYC" searches in 15 phrasings, ~2,557 suite searches (MSG, Knicks, Yankees, MetLife, Jets, "luxury suites", "suite rental"), ~2,021 group & work-outing searches (group activities, company and office outings, team building, birthdays, client entertainment). Similarweb volumes run below Google's on broad terms, so these are a floor. CPCs (Similarweb): suites $2.10–4.61, groups $4.67–12.63, "things to do" $0.30–0.99; the model uses $4.50, $5.00 and $0.85. AI visibility: Peec, ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, 92 prompts, 15 Oct 2026 (day one): the Jets are absent from 45 of 75 unbranded prompts, including 33 of 44 on corporate, group and family outings. Prices: TickPick cheapest seats for the six remaining home games $47–132 (median $62; Giants $86, Knicks $195); Suite Experience Group Jets suites from $9,469 (Giants $11,000), 15 Oct 2026. Attendance: Pro Football Reference, 2026. Google AI Mode answers checked 15 Oct 2026.
Formula: suite clicks = (ads × 1/6) ÷ CPC, capped at 15% of suite searches; suites = (suite clicks × 3% + suite searches × reach × 4%) × booking rate. Groups the same way with 3% / 4%, × tickets per group × ticket × 90%. Visitor orders = (ads × 2/3) ÷ CPC (capped at 15% of "things to do" searches) × click-to-order + "things to do" searches × reach × 1.5 × click-to-order; × tickets per order × ticket. Revenue a year = (suites × suite price + group tickets + visitor tickets) × 12. Revenue per $1 counts only ad-driven revenue. Revenue is gross ticket and suite revenue, before costs and food. Every rate and price is a placeholder until the Jets share their own; ROAST's fees aren't included.
