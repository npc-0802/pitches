What doing nothing costs

GayRealEstate.com's organic search fell from 7,544 visits a month in May to 1,063 in October (Ahrefs), and it's still falling about 25% a month. If that continues, it reaches ~250 visits a month by the spring buying season. At today's mix and the rates above, today's organic traffic is worth $25K a year in referral fees; at the spring run-rate it would be worth $6K. Meanwhile the two satellites send almost no one to an agent, and a peer that lived on directory search, Gay Realty Network, shut down on September 28, 2026, blaming changes in how search engines index sites.
