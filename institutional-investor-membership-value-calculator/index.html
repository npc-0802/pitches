What new manager memberships from search, AI and retargeting are worth to Institutional Investor

II's search traffic has fallen 47% since September 2025 (25,476 to 13,480 visits a month, Ahrefs) as the pages Google ranks fell 84%. Its 90+ events are largely missing from Google's AI answers and the conference calendars they quote. This models what fixing that, plus retargeting II's own visitors and a LinkedIn test, is worth in new manager memberships. Only the 21% manager share of readers is II's own figure; every other rate is a placeholder until II's numbers replace it.

What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers ~£43K of first-year membership revenue. II's search traffic is down 11,996 visits a month since September 2025 (−47%) and has fallen every month since June 2026 (Ahrefs). At the 12-month trend (−36%), another year takes ~4,800 more visits a month off search, worth ~£73K a year at these rates.

Assumptions & sources. Only the manager share of readers is II's own: 21% asset managers, 79% allocators, intermediaries or bankers (II readership survey, May 2026, in II's August 2026 digital sales deck). Every other rate, and the membership value, is a placeholder until II shares its own (membership prices by package, enquiries by source, enquiry-to-member rate). Search: Ahrefs, pulled 13 Oct 2026, subdomains, worldwide, modelled estimates: organic traffic of 25,476 visits a month at the September 2025 peak and 13,480 in October 2026; pages ranking in the top 100 fell from 11,662 (August 2025) to 1,894; Ahrefs traffic value of $26,172 a month on 16,949 visits, $1.54 (£1.17) a visit. Near-miss searches: 19 searches at positions 4–20 with 300+ US searches a month; event searches: 24 head terms (1,630 US, ~2,580 worldwide searches a month), visits at a standard click-through curve with a 50% AI Overview haircut. AI visits are an assumption. II publishes 140,000+ monthly visitors (about page) and 240,000 monthly users (sales deck); GA4 would replace the Ahrefs estimates. Ad costs are placeholders. Exchange rate: $1.3212 per £1 (13 Oct 2026). ROAST fees are not in the number.
Formula. Search & AI members = new visits a month × 12 × manager share × visit to enquiry × enquiry to member. Ad members = ad budget a month ÷ cost per click × 12 × click to enquiry × enquiry to member. Revenue = (search & AI members + ad members) × first-year membership value. Allocator sign-ups = new visits × 12 × (1 − manager share) × sign-up rate (counted, never priced). Revenue per £1 of ads = ad members × membership value ÷ ad budget a year. Each month of waiting = revenue ÷ 12. The tiers change only the visits and the ad budget; the rates stay as set.
