What waiting costs

Each month without the plan defers about $0 of new first-year revenue at these rates. The slide is already under way: HungerRush's marketing-site search traffic fell 72% in May 2026 and is still 61% below its April peak (29% down on a year ago). The pizza POS page, which sells to HungerRush's core buyer, has lost ~435 visits a month since April as Rezku and Thrive took "pizza pos system" and "pizzeria pos system"; at these rates that's about $0 a year of first-year revenue. Meanwhile 1,244 sites still link to revention.com, which sends no traffic, and the two OrderAI proof pages that Medium and ItsaCheckmate's "9 best restaurant POS systems" link to now return errors.
