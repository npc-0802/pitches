What defending and expanding search is worth

MarketBeat's Google traffic fell 71% since November 2025, from 6.24M to 1.84M visits a month, in line with a sector-wide shift to AI answers and big brands. The full peak isn't coming back for anyone. The plan instead protects what is left and wins clicks where they still happen: quote pages, non-Google search, and tools and calendars that AI can't replace. Set each lever, then MarketBeat's own newsletter economics.

DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Bing bring ~590K visits a month (a third of search) and each fell 17–24% last year. At −20% a year, ~118K a month is gone within a year. Bing Webmaster Tools, IndexNow and non-Google rank tracking slow that; stopping half of it is the working assumption.

Quote pages earn 151K visits (9% of Google traffic) despite holding the most links. Redirecting deleted pages to them and making them the hub alerts point to is estimated at +25%; +50% is the stretch. An estimate, not a measured rate.

~$309K a month goes to Google Ads, ~90% to one PDF sign-up page, and cost per visit rose 18% in two months ($2.26 → $2.67). Landing-page tests and bid caps on rising-cost list terms. Counted as cash saved, not added to traffic value.

What doing nothing costs

~118K non-Google visits a month within a year (~$89K a month in traffic value) if DuckDuckGo, Yahoo and Bing keep falling ~20% a year.

~3,700 referring domains a year, net: 13,509 lost and 9,792 gained last year (about −12%), largely from deleted pages that Forbes, Fortune, MSN and TheStreet link to.

~$56K a month more to hold today's paid volume if cost per visit rises another 18%.

One news alert carries ~636K visits (~$480K a month in traffic value) and fades when the story does. 77% of Google traffic now rides short-lived alerts; quote pages earn 9%.

Traffic value: Ahrefs US organic, Aug 2026 — 1.84M visits worth $1.39M in Google Ads terms, $0.76 a visit. Peak: 6.24M (Nov 2025). Motley Fool fell 61% over the same period; at that rate MarketBeat would have ~2.43M. Non-Google search and paid: Similarweb, US, Jun–Aug 2026. Tools and data sizing: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, Sep 24 2026 (position-3 click rates from Ahrefs, 40% off where AI Overviews show).
Formula: (non-Google visits kept + ticker gains + quote-page lift + new tool visits) × $0.76 = traffic value. Paid savings = $309K × % reduction, shown separately as cash. Subscribers = the same visits × sign-up rate; subscriber value = subscribers × 12 × value per subscriber. Traffic value (what the clicks would cost) and subscriber value (what they earn) are never added together.
Estimates, not forecasts: sign-up rate and subscriber value are placeholders until MarketBeat shares them; quote-page lift and non-Google retention are ROAST assumptions. Search Console access replaces these with real numbers.
