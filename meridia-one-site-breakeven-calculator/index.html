When moving Meridia’s 32 property sites onto meridialiving.com pays for itself

Meridia runs meridialiving.com plus 32 property sites, and each one has to earn its own place on Google and in AI answers. So as Meridia grows, ads carry more of the load. This compares two ways to hit the same growth target: keep today’s sites and buy the extra leases with ads, or move every community onto meridialiving.com in waves and let organic search carry more of it. Pick how to move, then replace our placeholders with Meridia’s own numbers.

Why one site

Eleven property sites and meridialiving.com each publish the same 549 apartment listings, so search engines see ~6,600 copies of the same pages, each marked as the original (sitemaps checked Nov 12, 2026). Seven towns have two or three Meridia buildings on separate sites competing for the same searches: Bound Brook’s three draw ~770 a month. In AI answers tracked Nov 10–12, AI ranked The Rail first in Bound Brook three times as often as any Meridia building and cited its site 3.5 times as often as Meridia’s. The next two buildings, Gloucester City and Toms River 40, sit on landing pages today; on one site they’d start with meridialiving.com’s standing instead of from zero. At these rates, one site needs $13,600 a month less in ads by month 24 for the same leases.

Waves: town pages, new buildings and the feed fix in months 1–3; wave 1 (Rahway, Garfield, Roselle Park, Lincoln Park: 6 sites, ~13% of organic visits) in month 4; wave 2 (the other stabilized communities: 23 sites, ~72%) in month 8; wave 3 (Pompton Lakes and Bound Brook: 3 sites, ~11%) in month 14, once both are full. “At risk” assumes Meridia stops after wave 1 if it shows no gain; moving every site at once would put $117K at risk.
Formula: target search leases = (organic leases + ads budget ÷ cost per paid lease) × (1 + growth target). Each month, ads buy whatever organic doesn’t cover (never less than half of today’s paid leases), and cost per paid lease rises with volume. Today’s sites: organic grows to the “same SEO effort” rate by month 24; every property site needs upkeep. One site: each wave’s organic leases dip when it moves and recover over the recovery months, while the gain builds over 12 months; 40% of the build cost lands in months 1–3 and the rest in the three months before each wave. Both paths run the same ads playbook and SEO fee, so those cancel out. Every figure uses placeholder rates until Meridia shares its own.
