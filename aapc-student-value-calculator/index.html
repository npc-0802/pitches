What waiting costs

At these rates, every month without the fix defers about $160,000 of first-year student revenue. Meanwhile AAPC pays ~$52K a month (Similarweb estimate) for career clicks on searches it already ranks top 3 for, Ahrefs shows organic visits sliding ~40,000 a month on average over the last six months, and the certification pages are down ~60% year on year. Similarweb already records an AI Overview on "medical coding", the one big keyword Ahrefs still sees without one.
